PSSI eigenvector maps · walking access to transit

Reading the colors: each map shows n × vᵢ, where n is the city’s section count and the right eigenvector sums to one. The arithmetic section mean is 1. The common logarithmic color scale spans 0.25–4 in every panel and city. Values outside that range use the arrow-end colors; observed minima and maxima (rounded for display) and saturation counts are printed on each panel. Zero numerical components use the below-range color and are flagged. Full-precision values remain in the CSVs. These are relative section scores, not citywide eigenvalues or normalized PSSI.

Four categories per city: White alone, Black alone, Hispanic any race and Asian alone. New York City retains its 70 sections; other cities use individual populated 2020 city-limit tract portions. Section size and geometry differ between cities. Higher colors indicate higher relative eigenvector components within that city/category, not a ranking of citywide segregation.
